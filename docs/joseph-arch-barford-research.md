## History — verified evergreen material
- Building listed **Grade II on 29 March 1983** (Historic England listing ID **1116495**), early/mid nineteenth-century architecture.
- Previously called the **Old Red Lion**; formerly connected with Flowers brewery, per CAMRA.
- Named for **Joseph Arch (10 November 1826–12 February 1919)**, born in Barford, pioneering trade-union organiser for agricultural labourers and later MP.
- His bicentenary was **10 November 2026**; the pub hosted a community celebration **7 November 2026**. Both dates are historical/past as of research date. Do not advertise as forthcoming events.
