# The Royal Oak · Evesham — research for prospect demo

Checked **11 November 2026**. Demo route: `/samples/royal-oak-evesham/`.

## Verified sources
- **CAMRA**, updated 3 November 2026: https://camra.org.uk/pubs/royal-oak-evesham-153856
  - Pub at **5 Vine Street, Evesham, WR11 4RE**.
  - Records reopening under new management on **31 October 2026**.
  - Describes a 16th-century black-and-white half-timbered building and says Charles I is **reputed** to have visited in 1645; do not turn this tradition into an established historical fact.
  - Opening hours listed: Monday closed; Tue–Thu 12:00–23:00; Fri–Sat 12:00–00:00; Sun 13:30–20:00. Operator should confirm.
  - Says real ale was not yet available as of the update. Do **not** advertise any particular beers.
- **Historic England**: https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1081337
  - Historic listing covers **4 and 5 Vine Street**, Grade II, first listed **7 May 1952**.
  - Notes facade modifications and some surviving interior beams; do not describe all exterior timbering as original.
- **Stonegate Pub Partners**: https://www.stonegatepubpartners.co.uk/find-a-pub/royal-oak-inn-evesham_025237
  - Describes public bar, open fires, dining area, catering kitchen, beer terrace, vaulted cellar and **six letting rooms**. Those are property features; after a change of management, active services and room bookings are **unconfirmed**.
  - Source of the interior and wide-angle frontage photographs shown in the demo.
- **Tripadvisor**: https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Restaurant_Review-g186422-d3483069-Reviews-The_Royal_Oak-Evesham_Worcestershire_England.html
  - Source of the authentic exterior close-up and older terrace/mural photograph. Older reviews, menus, food-service descriptions and opening hours may concern prior operators.
- **Public directories** corroborate **01386 442465** as a listed telephone number, but it is not independently re-confirmed for the October 2026 management: https://restaurantguru.com/Royal-Oak-Evesham and https://evesham.cylex-uk.co.uk/company/the-royal-oak-13204779.html.

## Image use in prospect concept
- All images are sourced from photographs of the **actual Royal Oak** (not stock illustrations or AI-generated interiors).
- The site currently **hotlinks third-party images** rather than bundling downloaded image files. The image host may change behaviour, block requests, add watermarks or remove assets.
- The Stonegate photos have a visible Stonegate lease/tenanted watermark from the original source. We have not removed it.
- Rights, source credits, and replacement with fresh client-approved photographs must be resolved before production use.
- The independent prospect concept is marked `noindex,nofollow`.
