# Customer handover

Give the customer two things: their website address and their editor address/login.

Explain the public website as a set of clear pages rather than one long landing page. Show them where visitors find the menu, opening hours, events and contact/visit information.

Show the customer how to change opening hours, switch an announcement on/off, edit a price/menu item, add or hide an event and upload/replace a gallery image. Show Save/Publish. Explain that one content update can feed the relevant public page automatically.

Explain that design, layout, navigation architecture, new pages and integrations are handled by the developer.

Do not train the customer on GitHub, repositories, JSON, deployment or hosting internals.
