# Customer handover

Give the customer two things: their website address and their editor address/login.

Show them how to change opening hours, switch an announcement on/off, edit a price/menu item, add or hide an event and upload/replace a gallery image. Show Save/Publish. Explain that design/layout/new pages/integrations are handled by the developer.

Do not train the customer on GitHub, repositories, JSON, deployment or hosting internals.
