Welcome to the Joseph Arch

A village pub
with heart.

Some places just feel like home.

In the heart of Barford, the Joseph Arch brings people together over thoughtfully cooked food, local ales and easy conversation. Reopened in spring 2026, the pub has a new chapter, led by a family who believe a great local should be for everyone.

Behind
the name.

Our name is part of the village's history.

Joseph Arch, born in Barford in 1826, helped change the lives of agricultural workers. His story, and the history of our Grade II-listed pub, are woven into the fabric of this place.
