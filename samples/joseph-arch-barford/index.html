THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PUB

A family.
A fresh start.

A village pub, brought back to life with heart.

Laura, Bradley and Harvey reopened The Joseph Arch in April 2026 after nearly a year of closure. They have worked to make it a welcoming place for the whole village — from the newly refreshed interior to the food cooked from scratch.

Behind
the name.

Our name is part of the village's history.

Joseph Arch, born in Barford in 1826, helped change the lives of agricultural workers. His story, and the history of our Grade II-listed pub, are woven into the fabric of this place.
