A warm welcome

Family is at
the heart of it.

New faces. The same village spirit.

In April 2026, Laura Dougan, her partner Bradley Pike and Laura's son Harvey Dougan reopened the Joseph Arch after the pub had been closed for almost a year.

With backgrounds in cooking and hospitality, the family rolled up their sleeves, refreshed the building themselves and set about bringing a traditional village local back to life.

Their vision is simple: good food, friendly service and a place Barford can feel proud to call its own.

Our heritage

The man
behind the name.

Joseph Arch (1826–1919) was born right here in Barford.

He became one of the leading voices for agricultural workers, helping to organise a movement for better pay, conditions and dignity in rural life. He later served as a Member of Parliament.

The pub, formerly known as the Old Red Lion, occupies a Grade II-listed building. In 2026 the village marked the bicentenary of Joseph Arch's birth — a reminder of how much local history lives on through a name.
