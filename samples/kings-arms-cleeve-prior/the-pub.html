Welcome

At the heart of
Cleeve Prior

The Kings Arms is a village pub and Smoke House in a historic stone building. Its current chapter began with a grand opening on 24 August 2025.

Locals and visitors can come together over food and drinks, right in the centre of the village. The building's stonework and timber features are part of what makes the pub distinctive.

The Smoke House serves smoked meats, steaks, pub favourites and Sunday roasts. The menus and prices are available online.

The building

Centuries
of history

The pub occupies a Grade II-listed building with sixteenth-century origins.

Its features include blue lias stone, timberwork and an inglenook, with a datestone dated 1691.
