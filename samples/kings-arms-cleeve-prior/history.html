An old village landmark

Centuries in
the making.

The Kings Arms is a Grade II-listed building with origins in the sixteenth century.

Its blue lias stone, timber details and historic construction make it a distinctive part of the village's heritage.

One of the most intriguing surviving details is a datestone inscribed WPA 1691 — a quiet reminder of the many lives and generations that this building has witnessed.
