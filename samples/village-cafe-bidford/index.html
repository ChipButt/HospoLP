Since 2019

Made for village life.

The Village Cafe started on Bidford High Street in 2019 and moved across the road to its current home at 48 High Street in 2022. Through changing times, the essentials have stayed the same: friendly service, good food and a place where people can stop and talk.

Read our story

“More than somewhere to grab a coffee — a little part of village life.”

Regulars, visitors, cyclists, families and dogs all make up the mix.
