Part of Bidford

A cafe is better when people use it.

Regulars, visitors, walkers, cyclists, families and four-legged companions all pass through. The Village Cafe has also become a home for local community activity.

First Saturday of the month

Bidford Clothes Swap

The cafe has hosted Bidford's monthly Clothes Swap over several years — a simple way to pass on good clothes, find something new-to-you and combine it with coffee and cake. Check current cafe or Parish Council updates for the latest time.

Dog friendly

Bring the four-legged regular.

Dog friendliness is one of the things customers mention again and again. The cafe welcomes dogs inside, making it an easy stop during a village walk.

Out and about

A useful stop for walkers & cyclists.

Independent cycling guides have singled out the cafe as a cycle-friendly stop, with breakfast, lunch, coffee and cake making it a natural High Street break.

Stay a while

More than one kind of seat.

Reviews describe different seating areas and an outdoor courtyard, giving people space for a quick coffee, a catch-up or a quieter pause.

What's happening?

Keep local updates close.

A live version of this site could pull current notices, special opening times and event updates into one dependable place instead of leaving customers to piece them together from different listings.
