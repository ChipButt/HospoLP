The story so far

From one side of the High Street to the other.

Proud to be part of Bidford-on-Avon since 2019 — and always happy to put the kettle on.

It started in 2019.

The first Village Cafe opened at 15B High Street, serving coffee, cakes, baps, sandwiches and plenty of home-cooked favourites.

When lockdown arrived, we kept going with takeaways and afternoon teas. In 2022, the cafe moved across the High Street to 48 High Street — the home you know today.

There are now several cosy places to sit, plus the courtyard out the back. The setting has changed, but the idea is still simple: good food, good coffee and a friendly place to meet.
