Our story

A little cafe with roots in the village.

The Village Cafe has been part of Bidford High Street since 2019. Its address has changed, but the warm, independent character people talk about has stayed remarkably consistent.

The Village Cafe at its current High Street home, photographed in April 2023 by JThomas / Geograph (CC BY-SA 2.0).

2019

The beginning

The Village Cafe opens on Bidford High Street, originally trading from 15B High Street. Early coverage already highlights Ingrid's home cooking, coffee, cakes, dog friendliness and a small courtyard.

2020–21

Keeping going

During the pandemic the cafe adapts with takeaway food, drinks and afternoon teas, staying connected with customers through a difficult period for hospitality.

2022

A new home

The cafe moves across Bidford High Street into its current home at 48 High Street. The property forms part of the Grade II-listed 44–48 High Street range, whose fabric includes much older historic elements.

2025

Five-star hygiene rating

A Food Standards Agency inspection on 6 March 2025 awards The Village Cafe the top rating of 5 — Very Good.

Today

Still a village cafe

Current reviews continue to return to the same things: a friendly welcome, coffee, cake, relaxed atmosphere, dogs and the people behind the counter.

The constant

Independent from day one.

Public records and customer reviews repeatedly name Ingrid in connection with the cafe. The business has the feel of an owner-led local cafe rather than a chain — personal, familiar and rooted in Bidford.
