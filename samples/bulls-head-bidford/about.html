ABOUT

A village pub in the heart of Bidford.

A long-standing High Street local with a relaxed, traditional atmosphere.

The Bulls Head sits at 36 High Street in Bidford-on-Avon and is listed by CAMRA and current local-business sources as a traditional village pub.

Current public information highlights draught beer and other drinks, live music and performances, sport, an enclosed beer garden and a dog-friendly welcome.

This concept keeps those reasons to visit easy to find, while the pub can keep current events and drink information up to date.
