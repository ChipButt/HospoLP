ABOUT

A family-run salon on Bidford High Street.

Hair, beauty, tanning and nails together in one local destination.

Finishing Touch is based at 82 High Street in Bidford-on-Avon and is described in current business listings as a family-run salon.

The salon brings four verified service areas together under one roof: hairdressing, beauty, tanning and nails. Customers can explore each area separately, then contact the salon for the current treatment menu, prices and appointment availability.

With weekday opening and a later Thursday evening, Finishing Touch gives local customers a convenient High Street option without travelling out of the village.
