A friendly atmosphere with plenty to do.

The Valkyrie is known for two pool tables, two dart boards, a jukebox and fruit machines, plus a programme of live music and community events.

The venue’s medieval-inspired shields and decorative swords make the stage and bar instantly recognisable.
