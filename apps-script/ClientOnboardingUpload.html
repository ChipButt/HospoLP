Your website

Original files give us the best results. Upload the files to their matching sections below. If something doesn't apply to your business, simply leave that section empty.

01 · Logos and Branding

Prefer SVG or a high-resolution transparent PNG. If you have official brand colours or fonts, include them too. Avoid screenshots of the logo.

02 · Photographs

Original camera or phone photos, ideally at least 2,000 pixels on the long edge. Show your premises, products, people or atmosphere. Avoid social-media downloads.

03 · Menus and Prices

Current PDF or original editable menu, price list or product sheet. Please make prices, availability and allergens accurate; avoid blurry photos of printed menus.

04 · Events and Information

Event posters and schedules, opening hours, contact details, social links or other information customers should know.

You may upload up to 50 files, maximum 25 MB each (250 MB total). Accepted: common images, PDF, Word, Excel and plain text. Do not include passwords, payment card details or confidential customer records.
